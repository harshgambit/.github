
# METAL

### Mother Engineering & Technology Applications LLP

**Technology-Driven Engineering | Industrial Equipment | Product Development**

METAL — Mother Engineering & Technology Applications LLP — is an engineering and manufacturing company focused on the design, development and manufacture of industrial equipment and engineering solutions.

Our work combines engineering design, product development and manufacturing to support industrial applications across multiple sectors.

- Industrial Equipment

- Product Design & Development
- Engineering Design Services

## Engineering & Product Development

METAL provides engineering support from concept and design through development and manufacturing.

Our engineering capabilities include mechanical product development, engineering design, manufacturing-oriented design, prototyping, testing and project execution.

We focus on practical engineering solutions designed around the technical and operational requirements of each project.
